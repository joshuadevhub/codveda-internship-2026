Begin Your Child's Journey

At Bright Future Academy, we welcome students into a supportive learning environment where academic development, character, and personal growth are valued.

Our admission process is designed to be simple and transparent, helping families understand the requirements and steps involved before joining our school community.

Admission Requirements

Please prepare the following documents before beginning the application process.

Completed Application

A fully completed admission application form.

Birth Certificate

A copy of the student's birth certificate or official proof of age.

School Report

The student's most recent school report card or academic record.

Passport Photographs

Two recent passport photographs of the student.

Application Process

Follow these simple steps to complete your admission application.

01

Submit Application

Complete and submit the online admission application form.

02

Pay Application Fee

Pay the required application fee of ₦7,700.

03

Assessment

Attend the entrance examination and interview.

04

Admission Decision

Check your admission status and complete enrollment if accepted.

Available Levels

We provide education across the following levels.

Nursery

Primary

Primary education from Grade 1 - 6.

Junior Secondary

JSS 1 - 3 with a strong academic foundation.

Senior Secondary

SSS 1 - 3 with preparation for higher education.
